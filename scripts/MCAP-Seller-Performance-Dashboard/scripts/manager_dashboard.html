Sources & method

Freshness, reconciliation and interpretation

How to read this briefing

  • The official reporting-period snapshot and the calculated closed-month comparison have different reporting bases. Neither is a forecast of year-end attainment. Reporting start and end are supplied by MINT, including YTD periods.
  • Official reported percentages are already percentages, not fractions. Official bucket weights are proportions. Portfolio percentages are never reweighted from filtered account dollars.
  • Official portfolio figures come directly from the supplied earnings performance data; the hero's score, weights and dollars are not reconstructed from account records.
  • Working-view bucket gap is max(sum of selected quota − sum of selected posted actuals, 0), calculated after aggregation, not a sum of individually clamped metric gaps. Metric surpluses offset shortfalls within the same bucket. The target includes any incomplete month; this is not a forecast.
  • Missing amounts are unknown, not zero. A bucket gap is unknown if any selected quota or actual is missing. Known actual/quota subtotals are explicitly identified when records are incomplete.
  • Buckets may overlap; do not combine dollar totals without verified credit rules. No combined dollar gap, quota or actuals total is produced across buckets.
  • Account weighted attainment is unknown. Account actuals/quota cannot establish a weighted attainment percentage.
  • Pipeline inventory is not verified incremental credit. Opportunity nominal USD estimates are not guaranteed TCV; monthly milestone consumption is a different unit. They are never added together or netted against remaining quota.
  • The optional ramp illustration is off by default and only permits known ACR-compatible metrics. Null source scenario inputs remain unknown even with account filters. It uses supplied milestone scenario credits, not verified earnings, and never changes actual remaining quota, official attainment or verified period credit.
  • Metric mappings are optional tentative hints limited to this user's plan. Unfamiliar metrics or unmapped production inventory require review: this data cannot certify zero metric pipeline. An opportunity is mapped only through linked milestone hints; unmatched inventory is not silently assigned to a metric.
  • The remaining-period window is today through official period end, inclusive. Overdue records never enter remaining-period candidate counts, monthly candidate rates or ramp scenarios.
  • A production candidate must exactly match “Production candidate - credit unverified”, be explicitly active in Production category, and have positive monthly consumption denominated in USD. An ineligible classification containing the word “production” is not sufficient.
  • Only Open opportunities enter open-pipeline counts and nominal USD totals. Won parent records remain visible as consumption context. Monthly milestone USD aggregates exclude other or unknown currencies without converting them.
  • Repeated record IDs or record numbers are counted once per record type. Opportunity and milestone counts are distinct inventories, not a combined pipeline count.
  • Large tables use a display-only row limit with “Load more”. All supplied records remain in memory, and counts, amounts and filters use the full matching set. Rows not yet displayed are not missing source data. Printed tables include only the currently displayed rows, with the display count noted.
  • The page uses only the injected in-memory snapshot. It makes no network requests and writes no browser storage. Copying or printing creates a separate sensitive copy under your control.
