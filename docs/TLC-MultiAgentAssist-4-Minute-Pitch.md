# TLC MultiAgent Assist: 2.5-Minute Pitch

**Audience:** General audience  
**Estimated delivery time:** 2.5 minutes maximum

Every sales opportunity has plenty of data. The challenge is turning it into a shared view of what matters, what is missing, and what happens next.

**TLC MultiAgent Assist** brings live MSX opportunity data together with Microsoft's customer engagement methodology in one role-aware workspace.

Select an opportunity and the team immediately sees:

**Where is the opportunity today? What is blocking progress? And who should act next?**

TLC does more than repeat the recorded stage. It tests that stage against evidence: the customer outcome, decision team, technical validation, business case, and next committed step. Each criterion appears as met, partial, or missing, with a rationale.

Four specialized assistants turn that assessment into action.

**Account Pulse** highlights urgent signals, risks, commitments, and actions.

**MCEM Coach** finds progression gaps and builds an owner-based plan.

**Pursuit and Executive Assist** prepares briefs, customer asks, and pursuit plans.

**Risk and Solution Play Assist** prepares mitigations, proof points, and objection responses.

Each role sees what it owns: Account Executives get priorities and customer alignment; Specialists get open exit criteria and owners; technical roles get validation gaps, dependencies, and delivery readiness; CSAMs retain context for adoption and value realization. Leaders gain a consistent view of health, risk, and accountability.

Most importantly, TLC is designed around trust. Recommendations link to evidence and citations. Missing information is called out, not invented. Users see only records they are authorized to access. Customer-facing content remains under human control: TLC can create an Outlook draft or Word export, but never sends automatically.

TLC is not another dashboard or chatbot. It is a shared intelligence layer that turns fragmented data into evidence-backed decisions and coordinated execution.

**One opportunity. One shared view. The right action, with the right owner, at the right time.**
