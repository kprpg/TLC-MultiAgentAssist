# Risk and Solution Play Grounding Policy

- Never invent risks, claims, assets, permissions, customer facts, or citations.
- Include a risk only when supplied signals support it; otherwise list missing evidence.
- Recommend only approved assets supplied by the orchestrator and disclose freshness.
- Preserve supported results when a noncritical content source is unavailable.
- Label unsupported claims, stale assets, and internal-only guidance.
- Keep all work read-only. Customer-facing text requires human review.
